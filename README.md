#ShopSmart Business Analysis

** Project Overview **

ShopSmart Retail Limited operates multiple retail stores across Nigeria. The company sells thousands of products across different categories and receives orders from thousands of customers every month.

Management has observed fluctuations in sales and inventory levels and requires actionable business insights to support better decision-making. This project involves analyzing ShopSmart’s business data to evaluate sales performance, identify trends, assess product returns, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

** Project Objectives **

The objectives of this project are to:

* Understand and clean the business dataset to ensure data quality and accuracy.
* Apply Excel functions, including lookup functions, to support data analysis.
* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Compare performance across product categories and store locations.
* Calculate and compare product return rates.
* Present findings using charts and an executive dashboard.
* Provide actionable, data-driven business recommendations.

** Tool Used **

Microsoft Excel

Excel was used for data cleaning, calculations, lookup functions, pivot tables, charts, and dashboard development.

** Project Deliverables **

The project includes the following deliverables:

* ShopSmart_Analysis.xlsx: Excel workbook containing the cleaned data, calculations, and supporting analysis.
* Executive_Dashboard.pdf: A visual summary of key performance indicators and business insights.
* Business_Report.pdf: A report presenting the key findings, analysis, and recommendations.

** Key Findings **

* Revenue Is Concentrated in a Few Store Locations. Lagos, Ibadan, and Abuja generated approximately ₦3.1 billion in revenue, accounting for nearly 49% of total revenue.
In comparison, Enugu, Owerri, and Kano generated a combined revenue of approximately ₦808 million, representing about 12% of total revenue.
This indicates that a significant proportion of ShopSmart’s revenue comes from a small number of store locations.

* Beauty Is the Strongest Category, While Home Has Room for Improvement. The Beauty category generated approximately ₦1.6 billion, making it the largest contributor to revenue. The Home category generated approximately ₦1.15 billion, despite containing the best-selling individual product. This suggests that there may be opportunities to improve overall category performance by reviewing its product mix, pricing, and promotional strategies.

* Sales Follow a Seasonal Pattern. December recorded the highest sales at approximately ₦590 million, while February recorded the lowest sales at approximately ₦510 million. This represents a difference of approximately 14% between the two months, highlighting a seasonal variation in sales performance.

** Business Recommendations **

* Prioritize Top-Performing Store Locations. Since Lagos, Ibadan, and Abuja contribute nearly half of total revenue, management should prioritize maintaining adequate stock levels, efficient operations, and consistent customer service in these locations. The company should also investigate opportunities to improve sales performance in lower-revenue locations.

* Build on the Success of Strong Product Categories. ShopSmart should continue investing in its strongest categories, particularly Beauty and Groceries, through targeted promotions and effective inventory planning. For the Home category, management should review the product mix, pricing strategies, and promotional activities to identify opportunities to increase sales across the category.

* Plan Ahead for Seasonal Demand. The company should increase stock levels and prepare its operations ahead of the December sales peak to meet higher customer demand. During slower months, particularly February, ShopSmart should consider targeted promotions and other sales initiatives to encourage purchases and help maintain more consistent sales throughout the year.

** Conclusion **

This analysis provides insights into ShopSmart Retail Limited’s revenue distribution, product category performance, and seasonal sales patterns. The findings highlight the importance of effective inventory planning, store-level performance management, and targeted promotional strategies.

By implementing the recommendations outlined in this report, ShopSmart can identify opportunities to improve sales performance, optimize inventory management, and support more informed business decisions.
